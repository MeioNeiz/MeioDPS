# Meio DPS

Damage and DPS on your spell, attack and weapon tooltips, and what each really did in your
fights.

![Shadow Bolt and Sinister Strike tooltips: before, only the game's text; with Meio DPS,
damage, DPS, damage per mana or energy, expected damage and the measured damage per
cast](docs/before-after.png)

## What it does

Hover a damage spell or attack and see what it does for you now, worked out from the
tooltip and your character sheet. After a few fights, the measured line shows what it
really did.

- Spells: damage, DPS per second of casting, DoT rate, damage per mana
- Weapon attacks (Sinister Strike, Mortal Strike, Aimed Shot...): your weapon plus attack
  power
- Attack, Auto Shot and wand Shoot: damage per swing or shot, and DPS
- Weapons: their DPS with your attack power
- Measured: real damage per cast from Blizzard's damage meter

Hold Shift for the maths, and on spells your ranks side by side per mana.

Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > MeioDPS, or `/meiodps`.

- `/meiodps casts`: everything measured so far
- `/meiodps forget`: clear the measured damage
- `/meiodps on`, `/meiodps off`: all tooltip lines
- `/meiodps help`: every command, each line on or off

## Install

Use your addon manager (CurseForge, Wago), or unzip a GitHub release into
`World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` and restart WoW fully.
